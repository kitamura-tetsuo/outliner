[Issues]
- The 'expandSelection' and 'shrinkSelection' methods in Cursor.ts were not mapped to any keyboard shortcuts, making the feature inaccessible to users.
- The default Shift+Alt+ArrowLeft/Right combinations were incorrectly triggering box selection.

[Changes]
- Updated KeyEventHandler.ts to map Shift+Alt+ArrowRight to 'expandSelection' and Shift+Alt+ArrowLeft to 'shrinkSelection'.
- Adjusted the box selection shortcut handler to only listen to ArrowUp and ArrowDown when Alt+Shift are pressed.
- Updated keyEventHandler.test.ts to test the new functionality.

[Verification Results]
- All tests in keyEventHandler.test.ts passed.
- E2E tests in slr-expand-selection-7d15b501.spec.ts and slr-shrink-selection-99167b65.spec.ts passed successfully.
